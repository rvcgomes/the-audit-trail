CFO & Head of HR, WeezieA

Venture-backed tech

Fractional finance, VCLevel

      
Clinics, wine, food and drink, PR agencies, and others

      
VCLevel puts senior finance, operations and people work into companies that don't need a full-time CFO yet. I take the finance seat: the model, the pricing, the cash forecast, the conversation with the bank.

      
Most of these businesses have never had a finance person look at them from the inside. A clinic doesn't run like a SaaS company, and a winery that holds stock for three years has a cash problem no startup playbook covers. That's the part I like.

Health & clinicsWorking paper

        
The question on the table

        
Will a second clinic pay for itself before the loan does?

        
What I build

        
A viability model month by month, with the loan, the hiring and the tax in it, and a price per chair-hour the team can actually use.

        
Start with what an hour costs.

      

      

        
WineWorking paper

        
The question on the table

        
How much cash is ageing in the cellar, and what does one more year cost?

        
What I build

        
A stock and working capital model by vintage, so the bank conversation starts from the right number.

        
Inventory is cash with a cork in it.

      

      

        
Food & drinkWorking paper

        
The question on the table

        
Which dishes and which locations actually pay the rent?

        
What I build

        
Margin by product and break-even by location, from the till data the business already has.

        
Busy isn't the same as profitable.
