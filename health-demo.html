Where I can help

Before the next big decision

The work is most useful before a decision that is hard to undo: a price list, a lease, a company.

You'll probably want to talk to me if

  • You've never worked out what an hour in the schedule is worth
  • You're not sure your prices for insurers and health plans still pay
  • No-shows worry you, but nobody has put a number on them
  • You're about to sign the lease for a new practice
  • You're choosing between sole trader and a company
  • You want to apply for public funding, or the incentives for the interior
  • You're bringing in a partner, or about to talk to the bank
  • Your team is growing and nobody has decided what each role is paid
How I work

An insider, working from the outside

I work with every part of the practice, from the front desk to the clinical team, so that each number turns into something someone does.

  1. DiagnosisWhere the practice stands today: what an hour in the schedule is worth, occupancy and no-shows, patients through insurers and health plans against private ones, and what each service costs.
  2. Thinking the business throughI look at the practice as a whole with the owners: the services, the prices, the team, and where growth could come from.
  3. Tools to decideBuilt for your practice: the pricing model, the break-even, the cash forecast, the monthly dashboard. With them I take the finance seat in the big decisions: the price list, the lease, the conversation with the bank.
  4. Pay and structureI design how each role is paid and the structure behind it: salary bands, roles, and the company structure.
  5. The next levelI get the practice ready for the next step, organic or not: a second site, a partner or an investor coming in, a merger or a sale.
